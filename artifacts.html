Artifact Usage: Rules & Examples

The Golden Rule: Association vs. Sequence Flow

Artifacts (Files, Databases, Notes) are **not** part of the process sequence. They are extra information.

  • GOOD: Use a Dotted Line (Association) to link an artifact to a task. This shows a relationship (e.g., this task *uses* this file).
  • BAD: Never use a Solid Line (Sequence Flow) to connect an artifact. This is a modeling error as it implies the "file" is a step in the process.
